Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about ordering, paying and delivery from the Elever Travel Shop.

How do I order?

Browse the shop, open an item to see its photos and details, and add what you want to your cart. At the cart, enter your name, email and phone number, then send your order on WhatsApp, or pay online if the Pay button is shown.

How can I pay?

If you order through WhatsApp, we confirm availability and share Mobile Money payment details. Where the Pay button is shown at checkout, you can pay online with MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money or card.

Is paying online safe?

Yes. Online payments go through Pesapal, a payment company regulated by the Bank of Uganda. You pay on Pesapal's secure page, and we never see or store your card details or Mobile Money PIN.

When will I get my order?

Items marked Readily available are delivered within Kampala immediately after we confirm your payment. Items marked Running on preorder are delivered within 5 working days (Monday to Friday) after we confirm your payment.

What does "Running on preorder" mean?

It means we order the item for you once your payment is confirmed. It is delivered within 5 working days (Monday to Friday).

What does "Readily available" mean?

It means we have the item in stock, so it can be delivered in Kampala straight after your payment is confirmed.

Do you deliver outside Kampala?

Yes. Please message us on WhatsApp, and we will make a different arrangement with you for your delivery.

Is there a delivery fee?

Yes. The delivery fee is calculated from the distance between our office (Plot 7, AHA Towers, Loudel Road, Nakasero, Kampala) and your delivery address. We confirm the fee with you when we contact you to arrange delivery.

Some items come in several colours. How do I choose?

Please choose from the colours we advertise on the item's page. Colours that are not advertised may not be available. Tell us the colour you want when you order, and we will confirm it with you.

Can I return an item or get a refund?

We usually do not accept returns and do not give refunds, so please check your order carefully before you pay. The exceptions are items that are defective or that are damaged or destroyed during delivery.

My item arrived defective or damaged. What should I do?

Contact us on WhatsApp +256 740 748 155 or at info@elevertravel.com. Give us your order reference and send clear photos of the item and its packaging. Upon our confirmation of the problem, we will arrange a replacement or a refund. See Delivery & Returns.

Who is Elever Travel?

We are a full-service travel agency based in Kampala, Uganda. We plan tours and safaris, book flights and hotels, handle visa guidance and airport transfers, and also sell handy travel essentials in our shop. Learn more about us.

How do I contact you?

WhatsApp: +256 740 748 155. Calls only: +256 200 924 856. Email: info@elevertravel.com. We are available Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm EAT, or use the contact page.
