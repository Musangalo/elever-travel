Delivery & Returns

How and when your Elever Travel Shop order reaches you, and what happens if something goes wrong.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Delivery

Every item in our shop is marked either Readily available or Running on preorder. How soon you receive it depends on which.

  • Readily available items, within Kampala: delivered immediately after we confirm your payment.
  • Preorder items: delivered within 5 working days after we confirm your payment. Working days are Monday to Friday.
  • Outside Kampala: please message us on WhatsApp, and we will make a different arrangement with you for your delivery.

After you order, our team will contact you on the phone number or email address you gave us to arrange delivery. Please make sure your details are correct and that someone can receive the order.

Delivery fee

The delivery fee is calculated from the distance between our office (Plot 7, AHA Towers, Loudel Road, Nakasero, Kampala) and your delivery address. We confirm the fee with you when we contact you to arrange delivery.

Preorder items

Items marked Running on preorder are ordered for you once your payment is confirmed. They are delivered within 5 working days (Monday to Friday). If there is any delay, we will tell you.

Returns and refunds

As a rule, we do not accept returns and we do not give refunds. Please check your order carefully before you pay.

The exceptions are:

  • the product is defective, or
  • the product is damaged or destroyed during delivery.

If your item arrives defective or damaged

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp +256 740 748 155 or at info@elevertravel.com.
  2. Tell us your order reference and send clear photos of the item and its packaging.
  3. Upon our confirmation of the problem, we will arrange a replacement or a refund. Refunds go back the way you paid, wherever possible.

How you can pay

You can order through WhatsApp, where we confirm availability and share Mobile Money payment details. Where the Pay button is shown at checkout, you can also pay online by MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money or card through our payment partner, Pesapal. We never see or store your card details or Mobile Money PIN.

Questions?

Message us on WhatsApp at +256 740 748 155, call +256 200 924 856 (calls only), or email info@elevertravel.com. We are available Monday to Saturday, 8am to 8pm EAT. You may also find your answer in our FAQ.
