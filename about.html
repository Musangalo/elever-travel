Who We Are

Luxury Travel,
African Heart

Elever Travel Management Limited was founded with one vision: to deliver world-class travel experiences anchored in the warmth and richness of Uganda. Based in Kampala, we are a licensed full-service travel agency serving clients from across the globe who seek the extraordinary.

We specialise in luxury, bespoke travel — whether you're planning a once-in-a-lifetime honeymoon, a high-stakes business trip, a family celebration, or an adventure into the wild heart of Africa. We handle every detail so your journey is defined only by wonder.
